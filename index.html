Platform Overview

What Is DataDive?

An all-in-one Amazon seller platform designed to help research, launch, and optimize products

DataDive is an Amazon seller platform designed to help sellers research, launch, and optimize products. It includes tools for product and niche research, keyword research, competitor analysis, listing optimization, PPC campaigns, and keyword rank tracking.

The platform also has tools such as Deep Dive, Master Keyword List, Rank Radar, Listing Builder, Product Scorecard, PPC Campaigns, and more. One thing I like about this approach is that you can use different tools for different stages of your Amazon business instead of relying on just one type of research.

What Is DataDive Used For?

DataDive can be used for several important parts of an Amazon FBA business:

Product and Niche Research

Product research is one of the main reasons Amazon sellers use DataDive. Its Deep Dive feature provides detailed information about a niche and the products inside it, including product images, sales and pricing information, technical details, logistics information, variations, and Keepa charts. This can help you study a niche before deciding whether a product is worth pursuing.

Keyword Research

DataDive also has a Master Keyword List that acts as a central keyword research tool for a niche. It shows keywords that competitors are ranking for, along with organic rank, sponsored rank, search volume, and relevancy data. This can help you understand which keywords are important in your market and how competitors are performing for them.

Competitor Research

Competitor analysis is another important part of DataDive. The platform lets you analyze products within a niche and compare information such as listings, pricing, images, variations, and other product data. This can help you understand what competing products are doing well and where there may be opportunities to improve your own listing.

Rank Tracking

DataDive includes Rank Radar, its keyword tracking tool. Rank Radar updates daily and combines organic rank tracking with PPC data, allowing you to see how your advertising activity and organic rankings are performing together. You can also track keyword rankings for individual product variations.

Listing Optimization

DataDive also has listing-related tools, including Listing Builder and Product Scorecard. These tools can help sellers compare their listings with competitors and use keyword research when improving their product content.

PPC

DataDive includes PPC tools for Amazon sellers. Its current product documentation lists PPC Campaigns, PPC Keywords, and PPC Campaigns for Launch. The platform also integrates PPC information into Rank Radar, making it useful to connect keyword research with advertising workflows.

Key Capabilities

DataDive Features

DataDive has quite a few tools, so I wouldn't recommend choosing the platform based on one feature alone. Here are some of the features worth knowing about:

Deep Dive

Deep Dive gives you a detailed view of a niche and its products. You can see information such as sales, pricing, product details, variations, images, logistics information, and Keepa charts.

Master Keyword List

The Master Keyword List is the main keyword research area inside a niche. It shows competitor keyword rankings, search volume, sponsored rankings, and relevancy information.

Rank Radar

Rank Radar tracks keyword rankings over time and updates the data daily. It combines organic ranking information with PPC data, which can be useful when measuring your overall Amazon visibility.

Listing Builder

Listing Builder helps sellers work on Amazon listing content using the keyword and competitor data collected in DataDive. DataDive also includes a Battle of the Listings feature that lets you compare your listing with competitors.

AI Copywriter

DataDive includes an AI Copywriter that is designed to turn keyword research into listing content. The number of AI Copywriter tasks depends on your plan.

Product Scorecard

Product Scorecard can be used to evaluate products using factors such as price, visual content, seasonality, variations, major brands, and written content.

Product Catalog

Product Catalog connects your Amazon Seller account data with DataDive. It provides a central place to see performance across ASINs, monitor inventory, track listing changes, view connected niches, and manage Rank Radars (requires active Amazon Seller account connection).

DataDive Profits

Provides accurate FBA fee calculations, landed cost analysis, and profit margin estimations so sellers can calculate net margins and ROI before committing to inventory.

DataDive API

Built for advanced sellers, brands, and agencies to export keyword metrics, competitor analytics, and rank history directly into Google Sheets, Airtable, or custom dashboards.
